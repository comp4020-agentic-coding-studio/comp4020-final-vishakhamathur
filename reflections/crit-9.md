# Crit 9

**What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?**

When I tried playing week 9 proof-of-life work, I found it felt very slow
and boring. There was no urgency for the players. There were multiple
threats at once which could be easily ignored by users. The users would
never really look at the same threat and this is something I did not want.
Also, the generator was looking confusing: titles and description picking
different names for the same alert. This took the trust away from users
before they could even vote for the threats. Then, I found out that both
these problems could be solved by shrinking everything down to one threat,
so all the users can focus on the same threat and put their preferences for
likelihood and severity and finally vote on it.

**What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?**

This work made me realise that software developers are not just coding
anymore, they are acting as supervisors for the AI agents. They are writing
harness and skill for the AI to implement software and then monitoring it
whether it follows the rulebook and prompting AI to not just implement
frontend but also the backend and manage huge databases. Also, now matter
what AI does, the human element of thinking, making choices and directing
the agent is never going to fade away.
